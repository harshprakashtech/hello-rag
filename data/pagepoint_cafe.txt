About Pagepoint
Pagepoint Books & Cafe is an independent bookstore and cafe in Sector 62, Noida. We opened in 2019 and stock over 12,000 titles in English and Hindi, including fiction, non-fiction, children's books, and competitive exam guides. Our cafe serves tea, coffee, sandwiches, and baked snacks, all vegetarian.

Store timings
The store is open from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. On Sundays we open from 11:00 AM to 8:00 PM. We are closed on Diwali, Holi, and Independence Day. During the annual book fair in December, we stay open until 10:00 PM.

Return policy
Books can be returned within 7 days of purchase if they are unused, undamaged, and come with the original bill. Opened stationery and cafe items cannot be returned. Books bought during sale events or at more than 40 percent discount are final sale and cannot be returned. Damaged or wrong books delivered online can be returned within 3 days of delivery.

Exchanges and refunds
You can exchange a returned book for another title of equal or higher price, paying only the difference. Refunds go back to the original payment method. UPI and card refunds take 5 to 7 working days after we receive the book. Cash purchases are refunded as store credit valid for 6 months.

Shipping within India
We deliver across India. Orders above Rs 599 ship free. Below that, shipping costs Rs 60. Delivery takes 2 to 3 days in Delhi NCR and 4 to 7 days elsewhere. Orders placed before 2:00 PM are dispatched the same day. You receive a tracking link by SMS and email once the order ships.

International shipping
We currently ship to the United States, United Kingdom, UAE, and Singapore. International orders take 10 to 15 working days. Shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on weight. Customers are responsible for any customs duties in their country. International orders cannot be returned.

Payment options
We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking, and cash in store. Cash on delivery is available for orders under Rs 3,000 in Delhi NCR only. EMI is not available. Payments made through UPI sometimes show as pending for a few minutes, and the order confirms automatically once the payment clears.

Membership programme
The Pagepoint Reader Club costs Rs 499 per year. Members get 10 percent off all books, free shipping on every order, and early access to book fair sales. Membership starts on the date of purchase and is not transferable. It can be cancelled within 7 days for a full refund.

Events and book club
We host a book club every second Saturday at 5:00 PM. Entry is free, and members and non-members can both join. Author talks and children's story sessions run on selected Sundays and are announced on our Instagram page. Seats for author talks are limited, and registration is required.

Contact us
